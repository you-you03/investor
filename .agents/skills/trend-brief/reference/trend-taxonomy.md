# Trend Taxonomy

Use this reference when proposing what terms and trends should be monitored. Keep the final user-facing report much shorter than this file.

## Monitoring Design

Track four layers:

1. **Macro regime**: market risk appetite and sizing constraints
2. **Sector rotation**: where capital is moving
3. **Catalyst narrative**: why a stock or group can move soon
4. **Execution risk**: whether entry is early, chaseable, extended, or blocked

Each watched item should answer:

- What changed?
- What ticker, ETF, index, or data point proves it?
- Does it create a trade candidate, a watch condition, or a risk warning?
- What would invalidate it?

## Must-Know Word List

### Macro

- **VIX regime**: market fear level that changes sizing.
- **Yield spike**: rate move that can compress growth multiples.
- **Dollar strength**: headwind for multinationals and commodities.
- **Credit spread**: stress gauge for risk appetite.
- **Market breadth**: whether rally participation is broad.

### Momentum/Technical

- **52-week breakout**: new high that can attract trend followers.
- **Relative strength**: stock outperforming market/sector.
- **Extension risk**: price has moved too far too fast.
- **EMA20 pullback**: common momentum re-entry zone.
- **Volume confirmation**: move backed by participation.

### Earnings/Catalyst

- **Guide raise**: management lifts forward outlook.
- **Beat and raise**: earnings beat plus higher guidance.
- **Analyst revision**: estimates or target price moving.
- **Multiple expansion**: valuation rises beyond earnings growth.
- **Event risk**: scheduled catalyst can gap price both ways.

### AI/Semiconductor

- **Inference**: AI model usage phase; supports chips, cloud, software.
- **AI capex**: hyperscaler spending cycle.
- **HBM**: high-bandwidth memory; AI server bottleneck.
- **Custom silicon**: in-house AI chips; can shift supplier winners.
- **Power bottleneck**: data center growth constrained by electricity.

### Risk/Execution

- **Chase momentum**: entering after confirmed move.
- **Early momentum**: setup before full breakout.
- **Stop breach**: price violates predefined risk limit.
- **Crowded trade**: consensus positioning raises reversal risk.
- **Thesis drift**: trade reason changes after entry.

## Suggested Scoring for Trend Priority

Score each trend 0-2 on five axes:

| Axis | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Evidence | anecdotal | one clear signal | multiple confirming signals |
| Urgency | no action | monitor this week | action today/next session |
| Portfolio impact | unrelated | watchlist/research relevant | affects open position or top candidate |
| Tradeability | vague | maps to ETFs/sectors | maps to specific tickers |
| Risk impact | low | changes conviction | changes sizing/stop/new-entry gate |

Priority:

- 8-10: report as top trend
- 5-7: include if space allows
- 0-4: omit or mention as background only

## Good Report Style

Prefer:

- "AI電力需要: データセンター電力制約が半導体以外の公益・電力設備にも波及。見る銘柄: VST, CEG, ETN"
- "金利上昇: 高PERグロースの新規エントリーは待ち。確認: 10Y利回りとQQQ相対強度"

Avoid:

- generic dictionary definitions
- long macro essays
- unsupported claims of market consensus
- trend lists with no action implication
